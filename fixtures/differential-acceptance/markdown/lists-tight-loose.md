# Tight and loose lists

## Tight unordered list

- Alpha
- Beta
  - Nested beta
- Gamma

## Loose unordered list

- Alpha paragraph.

- Beta paragraph.

  Continuation paragraph for beta.

- Gamma paragraph.

## Tight ordered list

1. One
2. Two
3. Three

## Loose ordered list

1. One paragraph.

2. Two paragraph.

3. Three paragraph.
